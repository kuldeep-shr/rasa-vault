About Rasa

Some things stay with you. Rasa is a place to keep them.

The songs, books, web series and movies that moved you are scattered across a dozen apps, and each of those apps is busy pushing the next thing.

Rasa looks back instead. It's one shelf for what already mattered to you, with a note on why. Over time, it starts to look like you.

Why the name? In Indian art, rasa (रस) is the feeling a work leaves in you. Not the plot or the cast, but the goosebumps. That's what this shelf keeps.
